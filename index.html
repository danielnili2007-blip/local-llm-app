local-llm

A private personal assistant that runs entirely on its owner's own computer.

What it does

local-llm is a self-hosted AI assistant used by one person, on their own PC. With the owner's permission it connects to the owner's own Google accounts so that, when asked, it can:

  • search and read the owner's Gmail and write email drafts (it never sends email by itself),
  • read and add events in the owner's Google Calendar,
  • find, read and create files and documents in the owner's Google Drive.

It is not a public service: there is no sign-up, and it only works with accounts its owner connects on their own computer.
